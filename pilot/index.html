White-Glove Pilot Setup

For our Ottawa pilot, our founder personally brings Lumina to your parent's home, sets up the system, and guides your family through the experience. Future retail versions will offer convenient delivery and easy self-setup.

What Every Pilot Family Receives

  • Personalized in-home setup. Jason (Founder & Engineer) personally installs and configures the Lumina Hub and radar sensing system in your parent's home (Ottawa area).
  • Caregiver app setup. Get started with personalized daily prose summaries, photo sharing, and incident alerts right on your phone.
  • Direct access to the creator. Enjoy ongoing support and share feedback directly with our engineering team.

Building the Future of Eldercare Together

We are inviting a small, dedicated group of families to join our private Ottawa cohort. This program is best suited for:

  • Location. Seniors living independently or semi-independently in Ottawa, Ontario.
  • Connected families. Adult children who live nearby or at a distance, looking for meaningful daily reassurance without invasive surveillance.
  • Technical requirement. A stable home Wi-Fi connection.
  • Partnership mindset. Families willing to share honest feedback through quick check-ins during the pilot period.

Transparent, Value-Driven Validation

To ensure every pilot spot is granted to families genuinely in need of this support and aligned with our ideal customer profile, we maintain a small commitment tier:

  • Fully refundable security deposit: $200. Covers the hardware suite. 100% refundable upon return of the hardware.
  • Special pilot subscription: $9.99 / month. Regular post-pilot rate: $29.99/mo. Includes all cloud AI processing, daily prose summaries, and ongoing app access.
  • Zero long-term lock-in. Cancel or withdraw from the pilot program at any time with zero hassle.
