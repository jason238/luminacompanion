Lumina Ecosystem Core — MVP-Ready Today

What we provide

  • A Lumina Hub, fully set up in your parent's home
  • A Lumina Wearable ring
  • Lumina Caregiver App
  • Free Senior and Caregiver accounts — no subscription during the pilot

Why is this free?

We're looking for a small group of families to help us improve Lumina before a broader launch.

What we ask of you

  • Openness to sharing feedback as we improve Lumina together
  • A short conversation or two about your experience
  • Patience — this is an early pilot, and things may not be perfect

Who this is for

  • Currently open to Ottawa, Ontario residents only. As an early pilot, we provide hands-on setup and in-person support, which means we need to be nearby.
  • Best suited for a senior living independently or semi-independently, with a family member who wants a clearer view of their day-to-day wellbeing.
  • A stable home WiFi connection is required.
