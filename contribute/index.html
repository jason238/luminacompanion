Contribute to Lumina

Help us bring Lumina to more families.

Lumina is building a new way for seniors and families to stay connected, supported, and understood. We're looking for thoughtful people who want to contribute their skills and be part of bringing this vision to life from the beginning.

At this stage, contributors can have a direct impact on how Lumina grows. You'll work closely with the founder, see your ideas come to life, and help shape the product, community, and stories behind Lumina.

Roles

Founding Growth Partner

Help build Lumina from the inside — operations, marketing, and growth alongside the founder. You'll get a close look at how an early-stage startup takes shape, and if there's a strong mutual fit, this could grow into a longer-term founding role over time.

  • Support day-to-day operations as we scale the pilot
  • Help with marketing and growth experiments
  • Contribute to startup building beyond any single function

Community Ambassador

If you're connected to Ottawa's senior-care community — through caregiving, a local organization, or your own network — help us reach families who could benefit from Lumina. You understand the people we're trying to help better than anyone.

  • Introduce Lumina to caregivers and community groups
  • Help refer families to the pilot program
  • Share honest feedback from the community

Storytelling Contributor

Help tell Lumina's story — through photos, short videos, and social posts that show what the pilot experience is really like.

  • Create short-form content (photos, video, social posts)
  • Help document pilot family stories (with consent)
  • Support early marketing efforts

What this looks like

  • Flexible — roughly 3–5 hours a week, on your own schedule
  • No fixed contract, no pressure — just honest collaboration
  • Direct access to the founder, not a layer of management

What you'll gain

  • Work directly with the founder — real conversations, not a hierarchy
  • Help build an early-stage startup from the ground up
  • Hands-on experience in community building, marketing, or storytelling
  • A personalized recommendation or reference for your contribution
  • The chance to help improve how seniors stay connected and understood

Interested in earning through introductions instead? Ask about our separate Affiliate / Referral Partner arrangement — a distinct, structured program for people who want to formally refer pilot families or partners, kept separate from these contributor roles.
