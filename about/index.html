About Lumina

No one should grow older feeling alone, or leave the people they love constantly wondering if they’re okay.

Why I Built Lumina

Like many adult children in their fifties, I live far from my mother. She often wanted someone to talk to, but I couldn’t always be there.

So I started building a conversation companion in my own voice for her, hoping it could be there when I couldn’t. At first, it worked well and brought her joy. But after a while, she gradually stopped using it.

That’s when I realized I had misunderstood what she really needed.

She didn’t just need advanced technology.

She needed a companion that could listen with empathy, remember what mattered, and help her feel seen, understood, and connected—even when family couldn’t always be there.

From that realization, the project gradually became something very different from what I originally imagined.

Fortunately, my 25 years building software, hardware, and applications gave me the experience to make this idea a reality.

But perhaps the biggest surprise came later: one day, I’ll probably need Lumina myself.

I’m not just building a product for today’s seniors.

I’m building the companion I hope to have one day.

— Jason, Founder

Lumina comes from the Latin word lumen, meaning light.

We believe that even a small light can make a home feel warmer, safer, and less lonely.

Lumina: Getting to Know You

Today, the core of Lumina (MVP) — Hub, Wearable, and Caregiver App — is real, working, and in the hands of early pilot families in Ottawa. What comes next is a fully connected, privacy-first ecosystem designed to support aging in place.

The Thinking Behind Lumina

Every meaningful relationship begins with small moments.

Sometimes it’s a conversation.

Sometimes it’s a shared routine.

Sometimes it’s simply noticing that today feels a little different from yesterday.

By themselves, these moments may seem insignificant. But over time—through memory, consistency, empathy, and gentle presence—they become understanding.

And understanding changes everything.

When someone remembers what matters to you, recognizes how you’re changing, and responds with care, trust begins to grow. Over time, trust becomes relationship.

That’s why Lumina was never designed as just a conversation companion.

Every part of Lumina—from voice conversations and long-term memory to wearable sensing and family connection—exists for the same purpose:

To better understand the person behind the interaction.

Because understanding creates relationships.

And relationships create something even more valuable than technology:

Peace of mind.

For older adults, it’s the comfort of feeling seen, understood, and never truly alone.

For families, it’s the confidence that someone they love is genuinely cared for—even when they can’t always be there.

That’s the thinking behind Lumina.

Small Moments ↓ Understanding ↓ Relationship ↓ Peace of Mind
