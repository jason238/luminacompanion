About Lumina

No one should grow older feeling alone, or leave the people they love constantly wondering if they’re okay.

Why I Built Lumina

Like many adult children in their fifties, I live far from my mother. She often wanted someone to talk to, but I couldn’t always be there.

So I started building a conversation companion in my own voice for her, hoping it could be there when I couldn’t. At first, it worked well and brought her joy. But after a while, she gradually stopped using it.

That’s when I realized I had misunderstood what she really needed.

She didn’t just need advanced technology.

She needed a companion that could listen with empathy, remember what mattered, and help her feel seen, understood, and connected—even when family couldn’t always be there.

From that realization, the project gradually became something very different from what I originally imagined.

Fortunately, my 25 years building software, hardware, and applications gave me the experience to make this idea a reality.

But perhaps the biggest surprise came later: one day, I’ll probably need Lumina myself.

I’m not just building a product for today’s seniors.

I’m building the companion I hope to have one day.

— Jason, Founder

Built on understanding.

Lumina learns who your parent is - not just what they said.

Through conversations, daily routines, wellness signals, and long-term memory, Lumina develops a deeper understanding of your parent's life. That understanding powers personalized support for your parent and meaningful insights for your family.

The Process | From Moments to Meaning

  1. Natural Interactions

    Your parent simply lives their day with Lumina—through conversations, video calls, gentle reminders, and everyday moments, while the wearable quietly stays connected in the background.

  2. Growing Understanding

    By bringing together conversations, wellness signals, daily behaviors, and long-term memory, Lumina develops a deeper understanding of your parent's routines, preferences, and well-being over time.

  3. Stronger Family Connections

    The Caregiver App turns that understanding into meaningful insights, helping families feel more connected, stay reassured, and share life's important moments—no matter the distance.

Lumina comes from the Latin word lumen, meaning light.

We believe that even a small light can make a home feel warmer, safer, and less lonely.

Small Moments. Lasting Understanding.

Every meaningful relationship begins with small moments.

A conversation. A shared routine. A familiar photo. A change from yesterday.

Individually, these moments may seem small. Over time, they become something more: understanding.

That is what Lumina is built around.

By remembering what matters, recognizing patterns, and staying connected to everyday life, Lumina grows to better understand the person behind the interaction.

And when understanding grows, so does trust.

For older adults, that can mean feeling seen, understood, and less alone.

For families, it can mean feeling closer, more informed, and more reassured — even when they can't always be there.

Small Moments → Understanding → Connection → Peace of Mind
