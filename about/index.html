About Lumina

Lumina comes from the Latin word lumen, meaning light.

We believe that even a small light can make a home feel warmer, safer, and less lonely.

Why I Built Lumina

Like many adult children in their fifties, I live far from my mother. She often wanted someone to talk to, but I couldn’t always be there.

So I started building a voice-cloned conversation companion for her, hoping it could be there when I couldn’t. At first, it worked well and made her happy. But after a few days, she gradually stopped using it. I upgraded it several times, but the same thing happened again.

That’s when I realized I had misunderstood what she really needed.

She didn’t need a more advanced AI chatbot.

She needed to feel understood—to have her thoughts remembered, her life stories preserved, and to feel cared for and connected with her family, even when we couldn’t always be together.

From that realization, the project gradually became something very different from what I originally imagined.

Fortunately, after 25 years building software, hardware, telecommunications, and application products, I had the experience to help turn this idea into reality.

Then I discovered something unexpected: in a few years, I’ll probably need Lumina myself.

I’m not just building a product for today’s seniors.

I’m building the companion I hope to have one day.

— Jason, Founder

Our Vision: The Complete Lumina Ecosystem

Today, the core of Lumina — Hub, Wearable, and Family App — is real, working, and in the hands of pilot families in Ottawa. Our long-term vision extends this into a fully connected, privacy-first smart home for aging in place.
