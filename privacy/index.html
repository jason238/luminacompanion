Your Privacy, in Plain Language

Lumina handles sensitive information — here's exactly what we collect, how we use it, and what control you have.

What we collect

  • Conversations: We do not intentionally retain voice recordings after processing. Speech is converted to text to power the conversation, and it's that text — not the audio — that we store and use going forward. (Some processing steps may briefly handle audio in order to generate that text, as is standard for voice-based services.)
  • Wellness data: Heart rate, SpO2, step count, and sleep data from the Lumina Wearable, and weight from the connected scale (if used).
  • Photos: Family photos uploaded through the Caregiver App.
  • Video calls: Live video between senior and caregiver. Calls are never recorded.

Who can see this data

  • Your parent (the senior using Lumina)
  • Family members you explicitly authorize through the Caregiver App
  • Our small team, only as needed to operate and improve the service — never for advertising or resale

Where your data is stored

Data is stored securely using Supabase, a managed cloud database provider. We are actively strengthening our data protection infrastructure ahead of pilot onboarding, including access controls that ensure only authorized users and systems can reach your data.

To provide voice and natural language features, processing may securely pass through vetted third-party cloud AI service providers. We ensure all platform providers adhere to strict data safety and privacy standards.

When you submit a form on this website — waitlist signup, Pilot or contributor applications, or contact forms — your submission is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree delivers the message to us by email so we can respond. Formspree’s own privacy policy governs how they handle that data while processing the submission; we do not use form submissions for advertising or resale.

Your control

  • You can request deletion of your data at any time
  • You can withdraw consent and stop using Lumina at any time
  • Contact us using the form below with any privacy request

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • Lumina is a wellness companion, not a medical device — we do not provide diagnoses or medical advice

Consent

Setting up Lumina includes a clear consent step covering voice processing, wearable health data, and (if used) video calling, in line with Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).
