Help Lumina Reach More Families

I'm building Lumina as an independent founder — and I'm looking for a few people who believe in this mission to help me get the word out.

Join as a Volunteer

Why we need you

Lumina is still small — one founder, a handful of pilot families, and a lot of work ahead. If you care about seniors living well and staying connected to their families, there's real, meaningful work here.

Roles

Community Ambassador

If you're connected to Ottawa's senior-care community — through caregiving, a local organization, or your own network — help us reach families who could benefit from Lumina. You understand the people we're trying to help better than anyone.

  • Introduce Lumina to caregivers and community groups
  • Help refer families to the pilot program
  • Share honest feedback from the community

Content & Social Media Volunteer

Help tell Lumina's story — through photos, short videos, and social posts that show what the pilot experience is really like.

  • Create short-form content (photos, video, social posts)
  • Help document pilot family stories (with consent)
  • Support early marketing efforts

What this looks like

  • Flexible — roughly 3–5 hours a week, on your own schedule
  • No fixed contract, no pressure — just honest collaboration
  • Direct access to the founder, not a layer of management

What you get

  • Free hands-on experience with the product from day one
  • A genuine reference / letter of recommendation for your contribution

Interested in earning through introductions instead? Ask about our separate Affiliate / Referral Partner arrangement — a distinct, structured program for people who want to formally refer pilot families or partners, kept separate from volunteering.
