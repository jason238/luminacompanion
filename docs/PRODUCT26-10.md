### Section 1: The Core Brain — Lumina AI Platform

#### Headline: An AI Companion That Truly Learns Their Story

**Sub-copy:**
Lumina is more than a voice assistant; it is a thoughtful companion that remembers past conversations, recognizes daily routines, and adapts to emotional nuances.

#### Key Features & Highlights:
* **Long-Term Context Engine:** Lumina gently remembers favorite stories, family members, personal preferences, and habits seamlessly across every interaction.
* **Dynamic Routine Insights:** By observing natural daily patterns, Lumina identifies subtle routine changes early, offering proactive comfort rather than clinical alarms.
* **Living Memory Book:** Everyday conversations are automatically woven into structured autobiography chapters, preserving precious family stories for generations.
* **Empathetic Speech Pipeline:** Delivers natural, warm, human-like voice responses with zero delay, speaking naturally without medical jargon or cold data outputs.

### Section 2: Lumina Hub — The Living Room Anchor

#### Headline: Effortless Home Companion. Just Touch or Speak.

**Sub-copy:**
Designed to sit comfortably in the living room, Lumina Hub is an intuitive, screen-optional interactive kiosk. There are no passwords to remember, no complex navigation menus, and no technical burden for seniors.

#### Key Features & Highlights:
* **Expressive AI Avatar Conversation:** Features a warm, dynamic animated avatar at the heart of the display that responds visually with lifelike expressions (smiles, nodding, gentle concern) to match the conversation's emotional tone.
* **Proactive Presence Detection:** Built-in mmWave radar senses when your parent walks nearby, enabling Lumina to offer warm greetings or gentle daily prompts completely naturally.
* **Senior-First Interface:** Features ultra-large, crisp typography (32sp+ font size), zero-scroll feature cards, and high-contrast visuals designed specifically for aging eyes.
* **Voice Calendar & Daily Schedule:** Effortlessly schedule family visits, community events, and social activities using natural speech:  
  * *"Lumina, remind me 45 minutes in advance that my daughter will visit me tomorrow at 4:00 PM."*
* **One-Tap Family Video Calls:** Seniors can start high-definition video calls directly with their primary caregiver with a single touch or simple voice intent.
* **Living Photo Frame & Photo Chat:** Displays family photos uploaded by loved ones, complete with warm AI-generated descriptions and interactive photo-driven conversations.

### Section 3: Lumina Wearables — Invisible, Layered Safety

#### Headline: Complete Protection Without the Bulky Hardware

**Sub-copy:**
Safety only works if it gets worn every day. Lumina replaces intrusive panic buttons with two sleek, non-disruptive wearable pieces that work together quietly in the background.

#### Key Features & Highlights:
* **Lumina Smart Ring (Day & Night Wellness):**
  * **Featherlight & Waterproof:** Comfortable enough to wear 24/7 to capture vital health signals, including resting heart rate trends, pulse rates, blood oxygen levels (SpO2), and sleep quality.
  * **Direct Local Sync:** Automatically syncs directly to the nearby Lumina Hub via direct Bluetooth — no smartphone or complex pairing required for the senior.
* **Lumina Fall & SOS Guard (Precision Safety):**
  * **Advanced 6-Axis Motion Sensing:** Powered by integrated accelerometers and gyroscopes to accurately detect high-impact severe falls and sudden posture changes.
  * **30-Second Cancel Window:** Displays a large, clear cancellation prompt on the Hub display to prevent accidental alerts before notifying family.
  * **Instant Hardware SOS:** A dedicated, single-press button instantly notifies designated family members during any urgent situation.

### Section 4: Lumina Caregiver App — Reassurance at a Glance

#### Headline: Meaningful Insights. Zero Data Fatigue.

**Sub-copy:**
Designed for busy adult children and caregivers, the Lumina App translates complex health metrics and daily moments into clear, human-readable insights. Feel closer and stay informed, no matter the distance.

#### Key Features & Highlights:
* **Daily Health Prose Summary:** Every morning, receive a clear, warm narrative detailing sleep trends, daily movement, and medication adherence.
* **Prioritized Incident Header:** Critical events (such as uncancelled falls, urgent SOS requests, or significant vital changes) appear as prominent, color-coded headers until acknowledged by a family member.
* **Shared Family Album:** Upload photos and captions directly from your smartphone to your parent’s Lumina Hub display in seconds.
* **Memoir Chapter Reader:** Read beautifully formatted autobiography chapters as Lumina helps your parent record their life history over time.
