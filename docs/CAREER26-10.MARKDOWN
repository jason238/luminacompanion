### Section 1: About Working With Lumina

#### Headline: Hands-On Experience in a Fast-Growing Health-Tech Startup

Lumina Companion (by IvyFeeder Inc.) is an Ottawa-based health-tech startup developing technology to help older adults age safely and comfortably at home while keeping families connected.

We periodically run short-term, project-based student opportunities designed to give university and college students real-world, hands-on startup experience.

#### What We Typically Look For:
* **Engineering & Technology:** Students passionate about mobile/embedded systems, IoT hardware, and AI development.
* **Growth, Sales & Community Outreach:** Communicators who enjoy connecting with local senior-serving organizations, families, and community partners.
* **Content & Storytelling:** Creative minds skilled at producing videos, product demonstrations, and engaging social stories.

*Applicants must be currently enrolled students at a Canadian post-secondary institution and be Canadian Citizens, Permanent Residents, or Protected Persons based in the Ottawa area.*
