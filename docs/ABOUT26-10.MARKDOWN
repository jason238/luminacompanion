### Section 1: Founder’s Story

#### Headline: Why I Built Lumina

Like many adult children in their fifties, I live far from my mother. She often wanted someone to talk to, but with distance and work, I couldn’t always be there when she needed me.

So I started building a conversation companion in my own voice for her, hoping it could stand in for me when I was away. At first, it worked well and brought her joy. But after a while, she gradually stopped using it.

That was when I realized I had fundamentally misunderstood what she really needed.

She didn't just need impressive technology or a clone of my voice.

She needed a companion that could listen with empathy, remember what truly mattered to her, and help her feel seen, understood, and connected — even when family couldn’t always be at her side.

From that realization, this project shifted from a simple technical experiment into something far more meaningful.

Fortunately, my 25 years building software, hardware, and applications gave me the engineering foundation to bring this vision to life. 

But perhaps the biggest realization came later: one day, I will likely need Lumina myself.

I’m not just building a product for today’s seniors. 
I am building the companion I hope to have in my own future.

**— Jason Zhang**  
*Founder & Engineer, Lumina*

### Section 2: Our Philosophy

#### Headline: Built on Understanding

Every meaningful relationship begins with small, quiet moments: a gentle conversation, a shared routine, a familiar photo, or a subtle change from yesterday.

Over time, these moments weave together into something far deeper: **Understanding**.

Small Moments  ──▶  Understanding  ──▶  Connection  ──▶  Peace of Mind

> *“Lumina comes from the Latin word **lumen**, meaning **light**.*  
> *We believe that even a small light can make a home feel warmer, safer, and far less lonely.”*
