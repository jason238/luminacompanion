This is more than a marketing project.

Lumina Companion is an Ottawa-based health-tech startup developing technology to help older adults age safely at home while keeping families connected.

We're currently running a localized MVP pilot in Ottawa and need students who can help us tell the story, create useful content and start conversations with the local senior community.

What You'll Do

Create Video & Social Content

Help us turn Lumina into clear, engaging stories that seniors and family caregivers can understand.

  • Film and edit short videos
  • Create tutorials and product demonstrations
  • Help produce testimonial-style content
  • Create social media content
  • Turn real pilot experiences into useful stories

Connect With Ottawa's Senior Community

Help us start conversations with organizations that serve older adults and caregivers in Ottawa.

  • Research relevant local organizations
  • Send emails and make professional cold calls
  • Introduce Lumina and its Ottawa pilot
  • Help coordinate demos, presentations or workshops
  • Support local pilot-user and testimonial outreach

All pilot-user, testimonial and community communications will follow Lumina's guidance and appropriate consent/privacy requirements.

You might be a great fit if you...

Applicants must be currently enrolled students at a Canadian post-secondary institution and be Canadian Citizens, Permanent Residents, or Protected Persons.

Required / strongly preferred

  • You can shoot and edit videos and can show examples of your work.
  • You enjoy creating social media content.
  • You're outgoing and comfortable talking to people you don't know.
  • You're currently based in Ottawa and can participate in local outreach.
  • You are proactive and can work independently.
  • You can commit approximately 60 hours between October 26 and December 18.

Strong assets, but not required

  • Connections with Ottawa seniors, families or community groups
  • Experience working with senior-serving organizations
  • Previous social media or marketing experience
  • Previous startup experience

Professional experience is not required. We care about creativity, initiative, communication skills and the willingness to get things done.
