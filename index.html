It gets to know your parent — not just what they said

Lumina doesn't just answer questions. It gets to know your parent over time. Every conversation builds understanding — routines, memories, preferences, mood, and wellbeing — so each interaction feels more personal than the last.

Why Lumina exists

Aging in place shouldn't mean aging alone. Many seniors spend long stretches of the day without conversation, and their adult children carry a quiet, constant worry: Is Mom okay right now? Lumina was built to ease both.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tap to talk

    Your parent taps the Lumina Hub — no wake words, no confusing setup.

  2. 2

    Lumina quietly keeps track

    Through everyday conversation and a simple wearable, Lumina naturally notices activity, rest, and mood — no check-ins, no chores.

  3. 3

    Family stays close

    The Caregiver App gives you a clear, calm "All is well" view — and lets you send love, memories, and messages back.
