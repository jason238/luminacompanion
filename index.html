Lumina AI Platform

The intelligence behind the companion.

Learns personal preferences, family relationships, routines, and conversation history to deliver increasingly personalized interactions and meaningful insights.

Lumina Radar

Ambient awareness. Without cameras.

Discreet mmWave sensing recognizes human presence, room occupancy, movement, and extended periods of stillness while respecting personal privacy.

Lumina Hub

A friendly presence in the home.

A simple, screen-optional home companion with proactive voice interactions, calendar reminders, family video calls, and shared photo experiences.

Lumina Smart Ring

Everyday wellness, quietly tracked.

A lightweight wearable designed to capture resting heart rate trends, pulse rates, blood oxygen levels (SpO2), and sleep quality.

Lumina Fall & SOS Guard

An additional layer of safety.

A discreet wearable with advanced motion sensing, qualifying severe fall detection, a 30-second cancellation window, and a dedicated one-touch SOS button.

Lumina Caregiver App

Your window into their everyday life.

Clear daily wellness summaries, important notifications, medication adherence information, family photo sharing, and access to personal memories.

Everything works together to help your parent live independently while keeping your family meaningfully connected.

More than technology. A more thoughtful way to care.

01

It Speaks First

Lumina doesn't wait to be called. It uses awareness of your parent's presence and daily routines to initiate gentle greetings, timely check-ins, and friendly reminders — making everyday life feel a little less lonely.

02

It Remembers What Matters

The more Lumina gets to know your parent, the more personal its conversations become. It remembers preferences, family relationships, and meaningful moments — turning everyday interactions into a connection that feels familiar and genuine.

03

It Notices Changes, Not Just Numbers

By bringing together radar-based activity awareness and wearable wellness insights, Lumina helps reveal changes in daily patterns, movement, heart rate, blood oxygen, and sleep — giving families a clearer picture of how their loved one is doing.

04

It Keeps Family in the Loop

Through the Caregiver App, families receive easy-to-understand daily summaries and important alerts, while staying connected through shared photos, video calls, and personal memories — even from miles away.
