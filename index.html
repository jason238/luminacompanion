Why Lumina exists

Aging in place shouldn't mean growing more isolated—or leaving families wondering whether everything is okay. Lumina brings together companionship, wellness awareness, safety, and family connection, so seniors can live more independently while families stay confidently connected.

How it works

  1. Natural Interactions

    Your parent simply lives their day with Lumina—through conversations, video calls, gentle reminders, and everyday moments, while the wearable quietly stays connected in the background.

  2. Growing Understanding

    By bringing together conversations, wellness signals, daily behaviors, and long-term memory, Lumina develops a deeper understanding of your parent's routines, preferences, and well-being over time.

  3. Stronger Family Connections

    The Caregiver App turns that understanding into meaningful insights, helping families feel more connected, stay reassured, and share life's important moments—no matter the distance.
