You worry if they are…

Traditional voice assistants sit in silence until spoken to, and fitness trackers only show cold, confusing numbers. Lumina is different. It’s an intelligent daily companion that grows to know your parent, speaks proactively when it matters, and keeps your family deeply connected—even from miles away.

How Lumina Solves What Others Can’t

Your parent talks with Lumina. You stay connected on your phone.

It Speaks First

Proactive Intelligence, Thoughtful Companionship

The Problem

Standard voice assistants require your parent to wake them up and navigate complex menus. If they forget, nobody talks.

The Lumina Difference

Lumina doesn't wait in silence. Powered by smart radar, it detects when your parent is in the room and within hearing range. It can offer a gentle morning greeting, welcome them home after an absence, or check in at night—at the right time and place, without requiring them to do a thing.

It Thinks Like Family

Dynamic Context & Long-Term Memory

The Problem

Ordinary chatbots forget everything the moment a conversation ends, treating your parent like a stranger every single day.

The Lumina Difference

Lumina’s true soul lies in its dynamic memory. Every conversation is shaped in real time by a living Context Snapshot—bringing together current health signals, daily routines, local weather, and personal preferences and habits learned through past conversations.

As Lumina gets to know your parent better over time, it helps turn everyday moments and cherished memories into a growing Diary. And as these stories accumulate, Lumina can help weave them into something even more meaningful: a personal Life Story, preserving the experiences, memories, and moments that make your parent who they are.

It Understands Trends, Not Just Data

Behavioral Insights & Real-Time Alerts

The Problem

A quiet day doesn't necessarily signal a problem. But an emergency can't wait for a pattern to emerge. Without context-aware monitoring and timely alerts, families are left guessing what's normal—and may miss what matters most.

The Lumina Difference

Lumina distinguishes meaningful changes in daily routines from ordinary day-to-day fluctuations. By establishing a long-term behavioral baseline, it recognizes when activity patterns or routines shift significantly, helping your parent check in gently and giving your family a clear, plain-language heads-up through the Caregiver App.

And when every second matters, Lumina doesn't wait for a trend. SOS alerts and qualifying health anomalies can trigger real-time notifications, helping your family respond when it matters most.

Effortless for Seniors

Simple, Hands-Free Care—with Help at a Touch

The Problem

Seniors may struggle with tiny smartphone screens, typing appointments, or finding contacts in an emergency.

The Lumina Difference

The Best of Both Worlds: Most of the time, no taps are needed—Lumina can initiate check-ins when appropriate. And whenever your parent wants to talk, a simple one-tap connection makes it easy to start a conversation.

Voice Calendar & SOS: Your parent can simply speak to add appointments, or press the integrated hardware SOS button to request help—without having to search for a phone.

Lumina is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Family SOS alerts notify designated family caregivers.
