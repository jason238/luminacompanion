Lumina

It gets to know your parent — not just what they said.

A companion that grows to know your parent.

Through conversations, daily interactions, and continuous wellness awareness, Lumina gradually understands your parent's routines, wellbeing, and what matters most—turning everyday moments into meaningful understanding that helps your parent feel understood and your family gain peace of mind.

More than conversations. Built on understanding.

Most AI remembers a conversation.

Lumina builds understanding over time.

By bringing together conversations, daily interactions, and continuous wellness awareness, Lumina gradually recognizes routines, preserves memories, and notices meaningful changes—helping families see not just data and status, but meaningful insights and tips.

Why Lumina exists

Aging in place brings freedom—but it can also create distance.

Families want to know their loved ones are doing well without constant check-ins. Older adults want to remain independent without feeling alone.

How it works

  1. Natural Interactions

    Your parent simply lives their day with Lumina—through conversations, video calls, gentle reminders, and everyday moments, while the wearable quietly stays connected in the background.

  2. Growing Understanding

    By bringing together conversations, wellness signals, daily behaviors, and long-term memory, Lumina develops a deeper understanding of your parent's routines, preferences, and well-being over time.

  3. Stronger Family Connections

    The Caregiver App turns that understanding into meaningful insights, helping families feel more connected, stay reassured, and share life's important moments—no matter the distance.
