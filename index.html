You call. They say, “I'm fine.” But how do you really know?

Between phone calls, a lot can change.

You want to believe everything is okay. But what if your parent falls when no one is around? What if their daily routine quietly changes? What if they spend another day feeling alone, or need help but can't reach you?

  • What if they fall and can't call for help? You may not know they're in trouble until someone checks on them.
  • What if something starts to change? A decline in activity, disrupted sleep, or unusual daily patterns can go unnoticed from miles away.
  • What if loneliness becomes their everyday routine? They may say they're fine, even when they miss having someone to talk to.
  • What if they forget important medication? You can't be there every day to remind them.
  • What if they need help and nobody knows? That's the thought no family wants to face.

The hardest part isn't always what has happened. It's not knowing that something may be happening — until it's too late to respond.

You can't be there every moment. But you shouldn't have to rely on an occasional phone call to know how your parent is doing.

More than a device. A companion who looks out for them.

01

It Speaks First

Because companionship shouldn't depend on someone making the first move.

Loneliness isn't always easy to recognize. And not everyone will reach out when they need company.

Unlike traditional voice assistants, Lumina doesn't simply wait for a command.

Using smart radar to sense when your parent is nearby, Lumina can initiate gentle conversations at appropriate moments.

A friendly good morning. A warm welcome home. A thoughtful check-in. A little encouragement throughout the day.

No wake words. No complicated menus. No need to remember how to operate another device.

And as Lumina gets to know your parent, conversations become more personal and meaningful.

A little more conversation. A little less loneliness.

02

It Remembers

Your parent deserves to be known, not just monitored.

A regular chatbot forgets. Lumina learns.

It remembers personal preferences, family relationships, daily routines, and meaningful details shared through previous conversations.

It can revisit familiar memories, display family photographs, and turn everyday conversations into a growing personal Diary and Life Story.

Your parent isn't simply talking to an AI. They're sharing stories, revisiting memories, and keeping family connections alive.

Over time, these conversations can become a treasured record of their life for children and grandchildren.

Because every life has a story worth remembering.

03

It Notices Changes

More than motion. A clearer picture of their well-being.

A quiet day doesn't necessarily mean something is wrong. But a significant change in someone's usual activity or wellness patterns may deserve attention.

Lumina combines camera-free mmWave radar awareness with wearable wellness information to build a more complete picture of your parent's everyday life.

Its radar can recognize presence, movement, and extended periods of stillness. The Smart Ring adds wellness measurements such as heart rate, blood oxygen levels, and sleep quality.

By learning your parent's normal routines and tracking relevant trends, Lumina helps identify meaningful changes that might otherwise go unnoticed.

When something deserves attention, Lumina can gently check in and provide your family with understandable updates through the Caregiver App.

No endless charts. No confusing numbers. Just meaningful information to help you understand how things are going.

Because knowing what's normal makes it easier to notice when something changes.

04

It Detects Falls

What if your parent falls and can't call for help?

This is one of the hardest things to think about when an aging parent lives alone.

Lumina's Fall & SOS Guard uses advanced motion sensing technology, including a 6-axis accelerometer and gyroscope, to detect qualifying severe falls and sudden posture changes.

If a potential fall is detected:

  • A clear cancellation prompt appears on the Lumina Hub.
  • Your parent has 30 seconds to cancel an accidental alert.
  • If the alert isn't cancelled, designated family members are notified.

And when your parent needs help for any reason, the dedicated one-touch SOS button provides another simple way to alert family members.

Because when you can't be there, knowing about a potential emergency sooner can make a meaningful difference.

05

It Keeps You Connected

Being far away shouldn't mean being out of their everyday life.

With the Lumina Caregiver App, you can stay connected to your parent's daily wellness and important events.

  • Daily health and wellness summaries in easy-to-understand language.
  • Sleep, activity, and vital-sign trends.
  • Medication schedules and adherence information.
  • Priority notifications for qualifying fall events, SOS requests, and significant vital changes.
  • Family photos that appear on your parent's Lumina Hub.
  • Simple family video calls to share everyday moments.
  • Access to personal Diary and Life Story chapters.

Instead of trying to interpret complicated data, you get meaningful updates that help you understand when things appear to be on track — and when something may need your attention.

Less guessing. More understanding. More meaningful connection.
