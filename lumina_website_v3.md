## PAGE 6 — PRIVACY (`/privacy`)

### Hero
**Headline:** Your Privacy, in Plain Language
**Subhead:** Lumina handles sensitive information — here's exactly what we
collect, how we use it, and what control you have.

### Section 1 — What we collect
> - **Conversations:** We do not intentionally retain voice recordings
>   after processing. Speech is converted to text to power the
>   conversation, and it's that text — not the audio — that we store and
>   use going forward. (Some processing steps may briefly handle audio in
>   order to generate that text, as is standard for voice-based services.)
> - **Wellness data:** Heart rate, SpO2, step count, and sleep data from
>   the Lumina Wearable, and weight from the connected scale (if used).
> - **Photos:** Family photos uploaded through the Caregiver App.
> - **Video calls:** Live video between senior and caregiver. Calls are
>   never recorded.

### Section 2 — Who can see this data
> - Your parent (the senior using Lumina)
> - Family members you explicitly authorize through the Caregiver App
> - Our small team, only as needed to operate and improve the service —
>   never for advertising or resale

### Section 3 — Where your data is stored
> Data is stored securely using Supabase, a managed cloud database
> provider. We are actively strengthening our data protection
> infrastructure ahead of pilot onboarding, including access controls that
> ensure only authorized users and systems can reach your data.
>
> To provide voice and natural language features, processing may securely
> pass through vetted third-party cloud AI service providers. We ensure
> all platform providers adhere to strict data safety and privacy
> standards.

### Section 4 — Your control
> - You can request deletion of your data at any time
> - You can withdraw consent and stop using Lumina at any time
> - Contact us using the form below with any privacy request

### Section 5 — What we don't do
> - We do not sell your data to anyone
> - We do not use your data for advertising
> - Lumina is a wellness companion, not a medical device — we do not
>   provide diagnoses or medical advice

### Section 6 — Consent
> Setting up Lumina includes a clear consent step covering voice
> processing, wearable health data, and (if used) video calling, in line
> with Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).

**Body:** Questions about your privacy or data? Reach out — we respond

## APPENDIX — Form → Backend Notes for Cursor

- All forms (Pilot application, Volunteer application, waitlist signup,
  About/Investor contact, Privacy contact) should POST to a lightweight
  backend or form service (e.g., a simple serverless function, or a
  third-party form handler compatible with static GitHub Pages hosting —
  e.g., Formspree, Getform, or a custom Supabase Edge Function endpoint,
  consistent with the existing Supabase backend).
- No calendar-booking integration (Calendly, etc.) — Jason will schedule
  calls manually via email.
- The waitlist counter (About page, Section 6) should be a simple
  editable config value, not hardcoded inline. The Ottawa pilot cohort no
  longer uses a live numeric spot counter — "Limited spots available" is
  static copy and doesn't need to be wired to a backend value.
- Do not include any personal phone number or home address anywhere on
  the site. Use a dedicated business email (e.g., hello@luminacompanion.com)
  for all contact points.
- The Privacy page wording has been deliberately softened around voice-data
  handling (see Page 6, Section 1) to avoid overstating a guarantee that
  could later be contradicted by the underlying API's own data-handling
  practices. Lumina currently uses OpenAI Whisper (speech-to-text) and
  OpenAI TTS (text-to-speech). Before onboarding real pilot families, it's
  worth a quick read-through — not necessarily a full legal review, but
  enough to make sure this language still matches OpenAI's current data
  retention practices at launch, and is updated if that provider ever
  changes.
